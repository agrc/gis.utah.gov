TURN GPS and NevadaGPS Users,

We are informing you of scheduled server maintenance and a GPS network software update taking place on **Tuesday, October 20th, between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m.**

Although we anticipate the network will only be down for approximately one hour during this window, please notify your field crews of the planned downtime and arrange for an alternative source of real-time corrections, if necessary.

Check our [status page](https://agrc-status.netlify.app/) for up-to-date information.

Thanks for your support.

GPS Network Support Team
